### Justify

Why a decision stands. Cite the file or the live editor value. Do not add a new design.

1. Name the decision and the artifact that should contain the reason (`source.json`, `design.json`, `score.json`, or a Resolve clip).
2. When the reason is in files, launch Astra in read-only mode. The answer quotes the field and the value.
3. When the reason is live Resolve, Blender, or UEFN state, read it on the parent MCP. Do not send that session to Astra or Opus.
4. Report the decision, the citation, and the measurement. If the artifact does not contain the reason, say it is missing.

A justification that needs a new render belongs to execute, not here.
